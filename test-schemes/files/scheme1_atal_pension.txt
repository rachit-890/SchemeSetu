Atal Pension Yojana (APY)

Atal Pension Yojana is a government-backed pension scheme aimed at providing income security to workers in the unorganized sector after the age of 60.

Eligibility:
- Applicant must be between 18 and 40 years of age.
- Applicant must belong to the unemployed, self-employed, or laborer occupation category (unorganized sector workers).
- Applicant must have a savings bank account or post office savings account.

Benefit: Guaranteed monthly pension ranging from Rs 1,000 to Rs 5,000 after the age of 60, depending on the contribution amount and age of joining.

Application Process:
1. Visit your nearest bank branch or post office where you hold a savings account.
2. Fill out the APY registration form with your Aadhaar number and mobile number.
3. Choose your desired monthly pension amount (Rs 1,000 to Rs 5,000).
4. Submit a copy of Aadhaar card and a cancelled cheque or passbook copy.
5. Provide auto-debit authorization for the monthly contribution amount.
6. Retain the acknowledgment slip and PRAN (Permanent Retirement Account Number) card.
