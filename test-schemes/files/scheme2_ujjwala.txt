Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana (PMUY)

Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana provides free LPG gas connections to women from economically weaker sections to promote clean cooking fuel and reduce health hazards from traditional cooking methods.

Eligibility:
- Applicant must be a female and the head of household or an adult woman member of a BPL (Below Poverty Line) household.
- Applicant must be a permanent resident of India.
- Annual family income must not exceed Rs 1,00,000.
- Applicant must be at least 18 years of age.

Benefit: Free LPG gas connection with first refill and stove provided at no cost, along with financial assistance for the security deposit.

Application Process:
1. Visit the nearest LPG distributor (Indane, Bharat Gas, or HP Gas) in your area.
2. Fill out the KYC form (Form A) with required identification documents.
3. Submit proof of BPL status or income certificate, along with Aadhaar card and bank account details.
4. Provide a passport-size photograph and address proof.
5. The distributor will verify documents and process the connection within a few weeks.
6. Track your application status through the distributor or the PMUY official portal.
