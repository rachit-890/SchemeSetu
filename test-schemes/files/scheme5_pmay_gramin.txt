Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin (PMAY-G)

Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana - Gramin provides financial assistance to rural households for the construction of a pucca (permanent) house.

Eligibility:
- Applicant must be a permanent resident of a rural area as per the applying state.
- Applicant must belong to a houseless family or a family living in a kutcha (temporary) house.
- Annual family income must not exceed Rs 1,20,000.
- Applicant's occupation must be farmer, laborer, unemployed, or self-employed (unorganized sector).

Benefit: Financial assistance of Rs 1,20,000 in plain areas and Rs 1,30,000 in hilly/difficult areas for construction of a pucca house, along with support for toilet construction under Swachh Bharat Mission convergence.

Application Process:
1. Contact your local Gram Panchayat or Block Development Office.
2. Check your name in the PMAY-G beneficiary list (based on Socio-Economic Caste Census data).
3. If eligible, submit the required application form along with Aadhaar card, bank account details, and income certificate.
4. A geo-tagged survey of the proposed construction site will be conducted by officials.
5. Upon approval, funds will be released in installments directly to your bank account, linked to construction progress.
6. Track your application and fund disbursal status through the PMAY-G official portal (pmayg.nic.in).
