Indira Gandhi National Old Age Pension Scheme (IGNOAPS)

IGNOAPS provides monthly financial assistance to elderly citizens from Below Poverty Line households to support their basic needs in old age.

Eligibility:
- Applicant must be 60 years of age or older.
- Applicant must belong to a household living below the poverty line.
- Annual family income must not exceed Rs 1,50,000.
- Applicant must be a permanent resident of the applying state.

Benefit: Monthly pension of Rs 200 to Rs 500 for applicants aged 60-79, and Rs 500 for applicants aged 80 and above, varying by state top-up contributions.

Application Process:
1. Visit the nearest Gram Panchayat office, municipal office, or Social Welfare Department.
2. Obtain and fill out the old age pension application form.
3. Attach proof of age (birth certificate, Aadhaar card, or voter ID), BPL card, and residence proof.
4. Submit the completed form along with two passport-size photographs.
5. The application will be verified by the local revenue official.
6. Once approved, the pension amount will be credited directly to your bank account or post office account monthly.
