Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana (SSY)

Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana is a government savings scheme designed to secure the financial future of the girl child, covering her education and marriage expenses.

Eligibility:
- The account must be opened in the name of a girl child.
- The girl child must be below 10 years of age at the time of account opening.
- A family can open the account for a maximum of two girl children (with exceptions for twins).
- Applicant (guardian) must be a permanent resident of India.

Benefit: Attractive government-guaranteed interest rate on deposits, with tax benefits under Section 80C. The account matures 21 years from the date of opening or upon marriage of the girl after she turns 18.

Application Process:
1. Visit any post office or authorized commercial bank branch.
2. Fill out the Sukanya Samriddhi Yojana account opening form.
3. Submit the girl child's birth certificate, along with identity and address proof of the guardian.
4. Provide two passport-size photographs of the girl child.
5. Make the initial deposit (minimum Rs 250, maximum Rs 1,50,000 per year).
6. Collect the passbook and continue annual deposits until the account matures.
